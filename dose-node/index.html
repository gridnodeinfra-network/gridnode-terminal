// HELP ME UNDERSTAND!

Some key concepts to help you understand your prescription, and how the dosage units are calculated. No math needed.

DOSAGE (MG)

The amount of medication you want to take, measured in milligrams (mg). This is not the same as the volume of liquid you need to inject!

VOLUME (ML)

The amount of liquid that contains the medication, measured in milliliters (mL). The dosage in a given volume is indicated by the concentration.

CONCENTRATION (MG/ML)

This is how much medication (in milligrams) is present per milliliter of liquid. For example, if the concentration is 10 mg/mL, it means there are 10 milligrams of medication in each milliliter of liquid.

UNITS

Units are another measure of volume, used to define the amount to pull into your syringe. On a typical (U-100) syringe, there are 100 units in 1 milliliter (mL). So 0.5 mL would be 50 units.
