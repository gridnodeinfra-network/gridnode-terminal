PRIVACY POLICY

Effective September 12, 2026

GRID//NODE is local-first. Your data lives on this device unless you choose cloud sync. This policy explains what is stored, where it goes, and the rights you always keep.

LOCAL-FIRST STORAGE

By default, everything you enter stays in this device's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, to anyone, until you connect a cloud account.

WHAT IS STORED

Your shot log (medication, dose, date, time, injection site, notes), weight entries, body measurements, side-effect observations, device registry entries, and app settings. All of it is user-entered by you.

OPTIONAL CLOUD SYNC

If you sign in with Google or email, your workspace syncs to your private cloud account for backup and multi-device access. Cloud sync is entirely optional — GRID//NODE works fully without it, forever.

YOUR EXPORT RIGHTS

Export your records as CSV or a complete backup at any time from VAULT → YOUR DATA. Your records are yours, in readable formats, with no lock-in.

YOUR DELETE RIGHTS

Delete all local data from VAULT → YOUR DATA at any time. If you use cloud sync, you can also delete your cloud account and its server copy. Deletion is permanent.

NOT A SOURCE

GRID//NODE does not sell, source, or facilitate peptide purchases. It is a tracking tool only.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

GRID//NODE does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosing guidance. Always consult a licensed clinician for medical decisions.
